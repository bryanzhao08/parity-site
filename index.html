How it works

Three steps, once a month.

Keep the scheduler you already use. Parity reads what it already holds.

  1. 1

    Connect bookings, logins and power

    Point Parity at your bookings, your instrument computers' logins and your power readings.

  2. 2

    Parity matches, prices and flags

    Each booking is paired with what the machine actually did, priced with your rules and charged to the right grant. Anything odd is flagged.

  3. 3

    Approve and export to your scheduler

    You review the flagged sessions. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

Booked vs used

A booking is half the story.

John booked the confocal for two hours. The machine ran for three and a half. The calendar can't see that. The power sensor can.

Parity bills what happened, and holds the extra 1.5 hours for you.

Everything stays on your computer.

Your bookings, logins and billing never leave the machine Parity runs on.
